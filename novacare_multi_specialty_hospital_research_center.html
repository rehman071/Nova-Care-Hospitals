About NovaCare Research Center

Setting New Benchmarks in Evidence-Based Healthcare

Founded in 2001, NovaCare Multi-Specialty Hospital & Research Center is a 500-bed quaternary care institution uniting multidisciplinary specialists, academic medical research, and next-generation surgical technology under one roof. Every patient receives an individualized care roadmap designed for rapid recovery and long-term wellness.

Our Mission

To deliver accessible, ethical, and life-saving clinical care enriched by continuous research, medical education, and unwavering empathy.

Our Vision

To be the region's most trusted destination for complex multi-specialty treatments, pioneering digital health and zero-harm patient safety.

When to Visit the ER Immediately

Chest pain, pressure, or shortness of breath
Sudden facial drooping, weakness, or slurred speech
Severe trauma, fractures, or uncontrolled bleeding
Loss of consciousness, seizures, or head injury
High fever (>103°F) with stiff neck or confusion
Severe allergic reaction (swelling of throat/lips)
