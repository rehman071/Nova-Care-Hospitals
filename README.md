# Nova-Care-Hospitals
🏥 NovaCare Multi-Specialty Hospital & Research CenterA modern, responsive, and interactive multi-specialty hospital and healthcare web application built as a college web development project. NovaCare brings together hospital information, specialized medical departments, a searchable doctor directory, online appointment scheduling with local persistence, a patient health hub with a BMI calculator, and a 24/7 emergency command center in a clean clinical interface.🌐 Live Website: https://novacaremultispecialty.vercel.app/📋 Table of ContentsProject OverviewKey FeaturesTech StackProject StructureGetting Started (Run Locally)DeploymentFuture EnhancementsAcademic / Project CreditsLicense🎯 Project OverviewNovaCare Multi-Specialty Hospital & Research Center is designed to provide patients and visitors with an intuitive, trustworthy digital gateway to healthcare services. The platform emphasizes:Rapid Access to Emergency Care: Always-visible 24/7 emergency helplines, ambulance dispatch, and trauma triage guidance.Seamless Specialist Discovery: Instant filtering across 8 clinical departments and 9 detailed specialist profiles.End-to-End Appointment Workflow: Interactive form supporting In-Person OPD visits and Video Teleconsultations, complete with instant reference IDs, printable/downloadable confirmation slips, and browser-persisted booking management.Preventive Patient Education: Medically styled health articles, an interactive Metric/Imperial BMI Calculator, and an expandable patient FAQ section.✨ Key Features1. 24/7 Emergency Utility Bar & Glassmorphism NavbarTop utility strip displaying 24/7 Emergency (+1-800-911-CARE), Ambulance Dispatch (+1-800-911-2628), Blood Bank (+1-800-555-0192), and daily visiting hours with one-click copy-to-clipboard toast notifications.Sticky glassmorphism navigation bar with smooth scrolling, mobile hamburger drawer, and a live My Bookings badge counter.2. Hero Showcase & Quick Specialist SearchAutomated crossfade medical background slideshow with slide indicator controls.Dual primary actions (Book Appointment and 24/7 Emergency Care).Embedded Quick Doctor & Department Search Bar that filters the specialist directory and scrolls directly to matching results.Hospital milestone highlights: 25+ Years of Excellence, 120+ Specialists, 24/7 Level-1 Trauma & ICU, and 500+ Beds.3. Hospital Information & AccreditationsInstitutional overview, Mission, Vision, and Core Values.Quality accreditation cards: JCI Accredited, NABH Certified, and ISO 9001:2015.Facility highlights covering 14 Modular OTs, Digital 3T Radiology, 24/7 Clinical Pharmacy, and 100-Bed Smart ICU.4. Specialized Medical Departments (8 Centers of Excellence)Interactive cards for Cardiology, Neurology, Orthopedics, Pediatrics, Oncology, Emergency & Trauma, Dermatology, and General Medicine:Clicking View Details opens a rich modal displaying the Department Head, specialist count, key procedures, and clinical facilities.Clicking Book Now pre-selects that department inside the appointment form.5. Searchable Doctor Profiles Directory9 Specialist Profiles with live availability indicators (Available Today / Next Slot Tomorrow), ratings, experience, qualifications, and OPD hours.Real-time keyword search and department filter pills with empty-state recovery.Detailed Doctor Profile Modals featuring clinical biography, education, awards, consultation fees, and spoken languages, plus one-click appointment pre-filling.6. Medical Services & Preventive Checkup Packages6 clinical service cards including ALS Ambulance, Advanced Diagnostics & Lab, Telemedicine Video Consults, Critical Care/NICU, 24/7 Pharmacy & Home Healthcare, and Preventive Screenings.3 Tiered Health Checkup Packages:Basic Wellness Checkup ($99 — 42 Parameters)Executive Comprehensive ($249 — 78 Parameters, Most Popular)Advanced Cardiac Shield ($349 — 95 Parameters)7. Interactive Appointment Booking System (localStorage Powered)Toggle between In-Person Visit and Video Teleconsultation.Dynamic cascading dropdowns (selecting a department automatically populates its specialists).Interactive Morning, Afternoon, and Evening time slot selector pills with client-side validation.Generates a unique Booking Reference ID (NC-2026-XXXX) and opens a Confirmation Modal with options to Copy Ref ID, Download .txt Slip, or Print Summary.My Bookings Slide-Over Drawer: Persists all appointments in localStorage so bookings survive page reloads, with slip download and booking cancellation support.8. Health Information HubFilterable Medical Articles: 6 wellness articles across Heart Health, Diabetes Care, Preventive Care, Mental Wellness, and Pediatric Care with full-text reader modals.Interactive BMI Wellness Calculator: Supports both Metric (cm/kg) and Imperial (ft/in/lbs) units with real-time score calculation, color-coded category badge, visual spectrum gauge, and personalized wellness tips.Patient FAQ Accordion: Expandable answers covering insurance/TPAs, visiting hours, lab reports, emergency admissions, and telemedicine preparation.9. 24/7 Emergency Command Center & Campus MapHigh-visibility emergency banner with 6 direct emergency helplines featuring one-click Call and Copy controls."When to Visit the ER Immediately" clinical warning checklist.Interactive SVG Campus Map highlighting Gate 1 (ER Bay), Gate 2 (Main OPD), and East Wing (Diagnostics & Pharmacy) with clickable gate directions.General Inquiry Form and Wellness Newsletter Subscription with instant toast notifications.🛠️ Tech StackCategoryTechnology UsedStructureSemantic HTML5StylingTailwind CSS (via CDN) + Custom Utility AnimationsTypographyGoogle Fonts (Plus Jakarta Sans & Inter)InteractivityVanilla JavaScript (ES6+)State & PersistenceBrowser localStorage API (novacare_appointments_v1)Icons & GraphicsInline SVG Icons & Custom Interactive SVG Campus MapHostingVercel📁 Project StructureThis project is architected as a lightweight, zero-build single-page web application for instant loading and effortless static hosting:novacare-hospital/
